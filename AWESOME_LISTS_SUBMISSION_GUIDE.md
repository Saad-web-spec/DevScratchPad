## 📋 Steps to Submit on GitHub in 2 Minutes:

1. Open any of the repository links above in your browser.
2. Click the `README.md` file, then click the **Pencil icon (Edit)** in the top-right corner.
3. Paste the markdown line in alphabetical order within the designated section.
4. Scroll to the bottom, enter the provided **PR Title** and **Description**, and click **"Propose changes"**.
5. Click **"Create pull request"**.
